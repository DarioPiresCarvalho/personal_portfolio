Welcome to my portfolio!

My name is Dário, a self-taught developer.

I'm a positive and deeply curious individual, passionate about solving problems and getting the job done while having fun. I want to create systems that work as expected without adding frustration for the end user.

What you see here is my first attempt at creating a a place where I can share my journey to becoming a software engineer. A place to share how I got here, what I've done so far to progress my learning, and future plans too.

My first priority is to gain a good foundational knowledge of web technologies such as HTML, CSS and JaveScript via the Foundations course of The-Odin-Project (more on this bellow) before I move onto the Full-Stack course and starting to build systems for problems I care about.

My Journey

I've always been curious about how things work and why they are done the way they are. Which is why I ask a lot of questions even about the little things. We've all used applications meant to make life easier, only to be frustrated with the experience because of the way they were designed. Encountering systems like this always left me with this powerless feeling, wishing I understood the design choices behind them.

That's where the interest to programme began, and it became what I wanted to pursue for the last couple years, but could never quite find the time for it. This year, with the support of my amazing partner, I'm finally taking the first step to achieving this dream!.

Gap Year

2017 - 2018

I worked various jobs in my gap year, picking up work in warehouse and kitchens to save as much as I could before I went to University. Thank god I did, because University turned out to be much more expensive than I realised.

The dream of building my own PC started here, and in my spare time I became obsessed with how computers were built. I had to settle for a Build Your Own 3D-Printer subscription and I started playing around making basic models in CAD software.

BA Business Management

University of Leeds

2018 - 2022

Dreaming of one day running my own business, I thought it couldn't hurt to know how to manage a business. If I learnt one thing during this time was how much of a people's person I was, as all the modules that resonated with me the most were always about the human-side of business.

Bartender

Estabulo Rodizion Bar & Grill

2021

In my final year at university, I had the opportunity to interview at a Brazillian restaraunt for a bartending role.

Despite the lack of experience in the field, the Bar Manager Antonio liked my enthusiasm to learn and attitude towards doing things right, and took a chance on me.

Beyond teaching me how to make cocktails, it was his approach to serving customers that I thought was the most impressive. No matter how busy it would get, he always went the extra mile to give every customer a unique and personal experience.

Bartender

Turtle Bay Hospitality Limited

2022 - 2023

With University finally finished, I took what I already knew and got another bar job.

This was quite the contrast from my previous role, and where before a team of 4 would suffice during peak times, now I was going to work alongside 9 others behind the bar.

If you don't know Turtle Bay, it is a popular bottomless brunch spot, where the goal is to get as many drinks as possible during your alloted time.

I got to work along-side some fantastic people, serving hundreds of cocktails every hour while having a fantastic time. An upbeat environment with positive people that made even the busiest of Saturday's a breeze in the park. Orders come at you fast, things go wrong, but together you get through it.
