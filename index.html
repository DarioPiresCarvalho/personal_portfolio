Welcome!

Hobby Maximalist

In 2026 I made my biggest career decision and quit my full time job that was completely draining me. I wanted to move into programming for a long time, and tried multiple times before to teach myself what I thought I needed to know in order to land my first role. After many failed attempts and rejections, I realised I couldn’t balance a full time job, my personal life and dedicate the time needed to learn programming properly.

With the support of my amazing partner, I took the leap of faith and quit work so I could dedicate as much time as possible into learning what made me happy.

I’ve just completed the foundations course from The Odin Project. I can’t recommend this resource enough to anyone starting out from the very beginning just like I am. After being introduced to HTML, CSS and JavaScript, I thought it was the right time to start working on a personal portfolio. A place that I can take all my. Interests and hobbies and apply the skills I’m learning to build a place that I can display these.

So a big thank you to yourself for stopping by and I hope you enjoy what I’ve built.
