My Journey

I'm a positive and deeply curious individual, passionate about solving problems and getting the job done while having fun. I want to create systems that work as expected without adding frustration for the end user.

What you see here is my first attempt at creating a a place where I can share my journey to becoming a software engineer. A place to share how I got here, what I've done so far to progress my learning, and future plans too.

My first priority is to gain a good foundational knowledge of web technologies such as HTML, CSS and JavaScript via the Foundations course of The Odin Project (more on this below) before I move onto the Full-Stack course and starting to build systems for problems I care about.

I've always been curious about how things work and why they are done the way they are. Which is why I ask a lot of questions even about the little things. We've all used applications meant to make life easier, only to be frustrated with the experience because of the way they were designed. Encountering systems like this always left me with this powerless feeling, wishing I understood the design choices behind them.

That's where the interest to programme began, and it became what I wanted to pursue for the last couple years, but could never quite find the time for it. This year, with the support of my amazing partner, I'm finally taking the first step to achieving this dream!
