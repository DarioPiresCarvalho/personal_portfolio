My Journey

We've all used applications meant to make life easier, only to be frustrated with the experience because of the way they were designed. I've always been curious about how things work, so encountering systems like this always left me wondering why they were designed that way.

Learning to programme has been an interest of mine that I really wanted to pursue for the last few years, but could never quite find the time for it. This year, with the support of my amazing partner, I'm finally taking that step!.
